# UI Language

BudWatch speaks like a thoughtful friend with excellent taste: warm, concise, specific, and never overbearing.

## Voice

- Use plain language and short sentences.
- Prefer “Find your mood” to “Select a discovery parameter.”
- Use confident labels for actions: “Explore film”, “Log experience”, “Try again”.
- Let the movie and the member experience carry the emotion.

## Naming

- “Experience” describes a member’s logged viewing context.
- “Crew” describes the trusted social circle.
- “Bud Score” describes the personal score for an experience.
- “Community Experiences” describes member context around a film.

## Empty States

Explain what is missing, why it matters, and offer one next action. Never shame a new member for having no history.

## Errors

Be specific enough to be useful without exposing implementation details. Provide a retry or recovery action whenever one exists.

## Content Boundaries

Do not imply medical, therapeutic, or guaranteed effects from cannabis. Avoid assumptions about consumption, impairment, identity, or lifestyle.
