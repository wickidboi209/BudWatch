# Product Principles

These principles govern product decisions when requirements are incomplete or competing.

## Feeling Before Filtering

Start with how someone wants to feel. Genre, year, runtime, and popularity are supporting signals, not the first question.

## Curated Over Crowded

Show fewer, better choices. A screen should make the next action obvious without becoming a catalog wall.

## Context Is Personal

Cannabis may be part of a viewing context, but it is never the identity of the platform or a required user attribute.

## Community With Care

Community signals should help people choose, not pressure them to conform. Design for warmth, consent, moderation, and useful disagreement.

## Identity Is Earned

BudWatch identity comes from logged experiences over time. Empty states should invite participation without pretending to know the user.

## Premium Means Intentional

Premium is expressed through hierarchy, pacing, typography, motion, and restraint. It does not mean adding decoration to every surface.

## Fast To First Value

The first meaningful movie choice should not depend on a long setup flow. Loading, errors, and offline gaps must preserve the user’s sense of progress.
