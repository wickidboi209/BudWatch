# BudWatch Product Vision

## Mission

BudWatch helps people discover the perfect movie for the kind of night they want.

Instead of asking:

"What movie do you want to watch?"

BudWatch asks:

"How do you want to feel tonight?"

The application recommends movies based on mood, atmosphere, community reviews, and shared experiences.

Cannabis is treated as one viewing context rather than the identity of the platform.

## Positioning

BudWatch is a social movie discovery product for people who choose films by feeling, atmosphere, and shared context rather than by title alone. The product combines cinematic discovery, personal viewing identity, and lightweight community signals in one calm experience.

BudWatch is not a streaming service, a cannabis marketplace, or a review database. It helps people decide what to watch and remember what the experience felt like.

---

## Design Goals

The application should feel:

- Premium
- Cinematic
- Beautiful
- Minimal
- Fast
- Fun
- Social

These qualities are product requirements, not decorative adjectives. Every feature should make discovery faster, make a choice feel more personal, or make shared viewing more rewarding.

Inspired by:

- Letterboxd
- Spotify
- Apple TV
- A24

---

## Core Experience

The Home screen should never feel like a boring list.

Instead, users begin by choosing a mood.

Examples:

😂 Laugh

🤯 Mind Bending

🌌 Sci-Fi

😌 Relax

👻 Horror

🎨 Animation

Then BudWatch recommends movies that fit that experience.

The primary loop is:

1. Choose a mood.
2. Browse a small, high-signal set of films.
3. Open a film for context and community experiences.
4. Log how the experience felt.
5. Build a personal identity and discover through friends.

## Audience

BudWatch serves curious movie watchers, couples, friend groups, and communities who want a better answer to “what should we watch?” The tone is welcoming to people with or without cannabis in their viewing context.

## Success Signals

- Time from opening the app to selecting a movie.
- Percentage of sessions that open a movie detail page.
- Experiences logged per active member.
- Meaningful reactions and comments per community experience.
- Seven-day return rate.

These are directional product signals. They should be measured only with clear consent and without collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the most enjoyable social movie discovery platform for shared viewing experiences.
